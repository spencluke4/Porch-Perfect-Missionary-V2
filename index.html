How it works

Three simple steps to order

Getting your porch setup is easy — just fill out the order form below with your details, and we'll take it from there.

1. Share the details

Fill out the order form with your contact info, the missionary's name and mission, your setup address, and preferred dates for drop-off and pickup.

2. Choose your options

Select your garage door size and material, and let us know if you'd like our front-door poster or if you'll handle that part yourself.

3. We confirm and deliver

After receiving your order, we'll confirm the details and schedule. Then we'll deliver and set up your porch display on your chosen date.

Common questions

Answers to help you order with confidence

Who is this service for?

Families planning a missionary send-off, homecoming, or any porch celebration related to a mission milestone in Utah County.

What areas do you serve?

We provide service throughout Utah County. If you're outside our typical service area, we may be able to accommodate you with an additional travel fee.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering at least 1-2 weeks in advance to ensure availability, especially during busy mission send-off and return seasons.

What if I'm not sure about my garage door material?

No problem! You can select "Not sure" in the form, and we'll assess it when we arrive for setup.

Can you accommodate special decor requests?

Yes! Use the special requests field in the order form to share any specific styling preferences, color choices, or custom details you'd like included.

How does pickup work?

Simply include your preferred pickup date in the order form. We'll coordinate with you to come back and remove all decorations on your chosen date.
